Minute Thé 1.0.1

- Active brewing sessions are now saved and automatically restored when you reopen the app, including the time spent away.
- Added a confirmation screen before stopping a brew, helping prevent accidental timer resets.
- Tailored the confirmation screen for Pebble Time 2 and Pebble Round 2.
- Improved confirmation-screen contrast in both light and dark themes.

Note: alerts are only delivered while the app is open. Restoring a session does not provide background alerts.
